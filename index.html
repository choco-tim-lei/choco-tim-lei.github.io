I'm a mathematics undergraduate at Imperial, on track for First Class Honours. My academic interests sit on the pure side — algebra, analysis, and geometry — with a longer-term curiosity around research in general relativity, particularly the geometric and analytical machinery behind it.

On the applied side, I'm equally drawn to building things — specifically in AI and robotics. I've worked on multi-agent LLM systems at Cambridge's Psychometrics Centre and on LLM-driven mobile automation at JoyArk in Shanghai, and I'd like more of my career to live in that direction: systems that act in the world, not just process tokens.

The two halves aren't as separate as they sound. Differential geometry underlies modern robotics. Optimisation and analysis sit beneath every serious ML model. I'd rather do both properly than pick a lane too early.
